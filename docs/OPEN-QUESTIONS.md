# Open questions

Only questions that are still undecided. When the owner answers one, record
a decision in `DECISIONS.md` that cites it, then delete the entry here.
IDs are never reused. Questions are ordered by when the build plan needs them.

Ask as multiple choice (D-018): options with a recommendation, more than
one pick where options combine, and the owner may always answer something
else entirely.

---

**Q-012 · How is dev mode opened, and does it ship?**
For example: a `?dev` address flag, a secret tap pattern, or both.
Should dev mode be in the public build?

**Q-013 · Text and UI font**
The system font, or a pixel font drawn in code (in keeping with "no font
files")?

**Q-014 · Other light sources**
Besides the lantern, what gives light (D-015)? For example: torches or
braziers in rooms, glowing creatures or plants, light you can carry or
throw. Can the player light them?

**Q-015 · Day outside**
The lantern isn't needed outside during the day (D-015). Is there a day
and night cycle, or are outside areas simply daytime?

**Q-016 · The lantern switched off**
Why would the player switch it off (D-015)? For example: it saves oil,
enemies notice you less in the dark, or both.

**Q-017 · Weapons, ranged abilities and area attacks**
Which kinds come first (D-017)? For example: a melee weapon and one ranged
ability for the first floors, with area attacks unlocked as you grow
stronger (pillar 4).

**Q-018 · Which biomes, in what order?**
The surface first, then down biome by biome, with caves that open into
valleys or pass through mountains (D-022). Which biomes, and how does each
portal depth relate to them?

**Q-019 · What kind of companion?**
One companion can join later (D-023). For example: an animal, a person,
something strange from the deep. How do they join, and do they fight?
