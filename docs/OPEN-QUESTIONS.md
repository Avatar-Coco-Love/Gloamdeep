# Open questions

Only questions that are still undecided. When the owner answers one, record
a decision in `DECISIONS.md` that cites it, then delete the entry here.
IDs are never reused. Questions are ordered by when the build plan needs them.

Ask one or two at a time. Offer options and a recommendation, but the owner
may answer something else entirely.

---

**Q-003 · Design pillars**
Three or four short statements that every later idea is checked against.
Draft them from D-008 and D-009 and propose them for approval. Don't adopt
them without it.

**Q-004 · What does oil do?**
Options to offer: light radius only; light plus a resource for actions;
oil as the run's clock or health. In style study 1 it is only the light
radius (1.6 to 5.2 tiles, a 2.5% drain per step, +45% per flask). Those
numbers are art-test values, not decisions.

**Q-005 · What happens when the oil runs out?**
For example: the light shrinks to an ember and enemies grow bolder; damage
over time; the run ends.

**Q-006 · Turns: does every step take a turn?**
Do enemies act only when you act (classic roguelike), or on a timer?

**Q-007 · Fighting: how does it work?**
For example: walk into an enemy to attack; abilities; using the lantern
itself as a weapon.

**Q-008 · The shape of a run**
Permadeath runs that start fresh, or a persistent journey? Do floors go
down, biome by biome? What carries over between runs?

**Q-009 · Biomes, outside and inside**
The owner wanted to see biomes and both outside and inside areas. Which
biomes, in what order, and how do outside and inside connect?

**Q-010 · Companions?**
Dungeon Siege had a party. Solo, one companion, or a party?

**Q-012 · How is dev mode opened, and does it ship?**
For example: a `?dev` address flag, a secret tap pattern, or both.
Should dev mode be in the public build?

**Q-013 · Text and UI font**
The system font, or a pixel font drawn in code (in keeping with "no font
files")?
