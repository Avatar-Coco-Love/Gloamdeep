What this study tries

  • The lantern is real light. It casts shadows behind pillars, shrinks as oil runs low, and flickers.
  • Fog of war. Rooms start black. Places you have seen stay faintly visible.
  • Walls with faces. A slight top-down tilt shows brick fronts and casts shade on the floor.
  • 16-pixel tiles, enlarged. Every pixel is drawn by code. No image files.
  • Small life. Torches sway, dust drifts in the light, monsters bob, and walking into one makes it flash.

On a keyboard: arrow keys or WASD to step, L to toggle lighting.
